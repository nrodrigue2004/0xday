# things to try


    

  1. Straight line, easy data. Two blobs plus logistic regression reaches near-perfect accuracy in a few dozen epochs. Watch the loss fall.
    2. 

  2. The model that cannot bend. Switch to rings or xor with logistic regression. No amount of training helps, because a straight line cannot separate them. This is underfitting: the model is too simple.
    3. 

  3. Add capacity. Keep the rings and switch to the neural net. Train a few hundred epochs: the boundary curves around the inner ring.
    4. 

  4. Break the learning rate. Set it to the maximum and train the net. Loss may bounce or stall. Too small and it crawls.
    5. 

  5. The accuracy trap. Drop the malicious share to 5 percent. Accuracy stays high while recall (shown in the stats) falls. Move the threshold down and watch recall rise and precision fall.
    6. 

  6. Overfit. Use moons with noise 0.4 and train the net for 1000+ epochs. Compare train accuracy with test accuracy: the gap is overfitting.
    7.
