quantum and PQC terms

qubit
quantum bit; holds a blend of 0 and 1 until measured
entanglement
linked qubits whose results are correlated
Shor
quantum algorithm that breaks RSA, DH and ECC
Grover
quantum search speedup; halves effective symmetric key strength
CRQC
cryptographically relevant quantum computer: one big enough to break real keys
PQC
post-quantum cryptography: classical algorithms believed quantum-resistant
ML-KEM / ML-DSA / SLH-DSA
NIST FIPS 203 / 204 / 205 (2024)
HNDL
harvest now, decrypt later
crypto agility
being able to swap algorithms without a rebuild
hybrid
classical + PQC key exchange together

malware families

virus
attaches to files or boot sectors; needs a host and often a user action
worm
copies itself across a network without a user
trojan
looks legitimate, does something else
ransomware
encrypts or steals data and demands payment
botnet
many compromised devices controlled together
rootkit
hides malware from the OS and tools
backdoor
hidden access path, planted or built in
zero-day
a flaw the vendor does not know about yet
n-day
a known, patched flaw that organisations have not patched
dwell time
time between compromise and detection
