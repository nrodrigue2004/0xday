at a glance

Date
2 November 1988
Author
Robert Tappan Morris (Cornell student; launched from MIT)
Scale
roughly 6,000 machines, commonly cited as about 10% of the internet then
Bug class
stack buffer overflow (CWE-121), also CWE-242: use of an inherently dangerous function
Entry points
fingerd gets(), sendmail DEBUG, rsh/rexec trust, weak passwords
Fix
bounded reads (fgets), disable DEBUG, remove trust, shadow passwords
Aftermath
CERT/CC; first CFAA conviction
