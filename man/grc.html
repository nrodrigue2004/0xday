nist csf 2.0

Govern
strategy, roles, policy, oversight
Identify
assets, risk, improvement
Protect
access, training, data security, platform security
Detect
continuous monitoring, adverse event analysis
Respond
incident management, analysis, reporting, mitigation
Recover
recovery plan execution, communication

800-53 families

AC AT AU CA CM CP
access, training, audit, assessment, config, contingency
IA IR MA MP PE PL
authentication, incident response, maintenance, media, physical, planning
PM PS PT RA SA SC SI SR
program mgmt, personnel, PII, risk, acquisition, comms protection, integrity, supply chain

control types

Preventive
stops it (firewall, MFA)
Detective
notices it (SIEM, camera)
Corrective
fixes it (restore, patch)
Deterrent
discourages it (warning banner)
Compensating
substitutes when the main control is not possible
Directive
tells people what to do (policy)

terms

ISMS
information security management system (ISO 27001)
SoA
statement of applicability
ATO
authority to operate
Crosswalk
mapping between frameworks
Gap assessment
compare current state to a target

formulas

SLE = AV x EF
loss from one event
ALE = SLE x ARO
expected loss per year
Control value = ALE before - ALE after - yearly cost
positive means worth buying
Risk score = likelihood x impact
qualitative ranking
Residual risk = inherent risk - controls
what remains

responses

Mitigate
reduce with controls
Transfer
insurance, contract, outsource
Accept
knowingly keep, with sign-off
Avoid
stop the activity

metrics

RTO
how fast a process must be back
RPO
how much data loss is tolerable
MTTR
mean time to repair
MTBF
mean time between failures
KRI
key risk indicator

hierarchy

Policy
leadership statement of required behavior and why
Standard
mandatory, measurable specification
Procedure
step-by-step instructions
Guideline
recommended practice, not mandatory
Baseline
minimum configuration for a type of system

Purpose, Scope
why and who/what it covers
Statements
numbered, testable requirements
Roles
owners and responsibilities
Exceptions
request, approval, expiry
Enforcement
consequences of non-compliance

common policies

AUP

IRP

BCP / DRP
business continuity / disaster recovery
BYOD

Data classification
levels and handling rules

standards

SOC 2
AICPA report on security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy
SOC 2 Type I / II
design at a point in time / operating over a period
PCI DSS
card data standard; 12 principal requirements
HIPAA
US health data law: Privacy, Security, Breach Notification rules
SOX
US financial reporting controls for public companies
GLBA
US financial institutions' customer data safeguards
ISO 27001
certifiable ISMS standard

audit terms

Evidence
dated, attributable proof a control works
Population / sample
all items / the subset tested
Exception
a sampled item that failed
Finding
a control gap reported
Design vs operating effectiveness
is it right / did it work every time
Attestation
formal statement of assurance

roles

QSA
PCI qualified security assessor
BAA
HIPAA business associate agreement
CISA
certified information systems auditor
Three lines of defense
operate, oversee, assure

laws

GDPR
EU: 72 h breach notice to regulator; fines to 20M euros or 4% revenue
CCPA / CPRA
California: know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing
HIPAA
US health data: individual notice within 60 days
FERPA
US student records
GLBA
US financial customer data
COPPA
US children under 13, parental consent

gdpr rights

Access
get a copy of your data
Rectification
correct inaccurate data
Erasure
delete (with exceptions)
Portability
take your data to another provider
Objection
stop certain processing
Restriction
pause processing

codes

(ISC)2 canons
protect society, act honorably, provide diligent service, advance the profession
ACM Code
contribute to society, avoid harm, be honest, respect privacy, honor confidentiality
Authorization
written permission, scope, and dates
Least data
take the minimum evidence needed

disclosure

VDP

Bug bounty
paid program with scope and rules
Coordinated disclosure
report privately, fix, then publish together
Full disclosure
publish everything immediately
90 days
common deadline norm before publication
CVE

CERT/CC
coordination center that can mediate reports

anti-circumvention rule with security research exemptions

policy language promising not to pursue good-faith researchers
