Programming languages, how compilers work, and the theory underneath. The three courses it feeds are CS340, CS310 and CS301. Each chapter goes simple, mid, complex and ends in a hands-on lab, most of them real Python that runs in your browser.

progress: [####] = all levels read and the lab solved. Saved in your browser. Courses with quizzes and exams: ~/courses.

Why a security person cares: parsers are where input becomes behavior, and most injection bugs are a parser confusion. Reverse engineering is decompiling what a compiler did. Detection rules and regexes are automata. Pair it with ~/code for the practical scripting side and ~/rev for what the compiler's output looks like.
