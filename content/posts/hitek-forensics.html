The HiTek challenge handed us a Windows disk image, an Ubuntu disk image, and a network capture, and asked one question: what happened, and who did it?

Tools

  • FTK Imager to mount and verify the images
  • Autopsy for the file system timeline, browser history and deleted files
  • Wireshark to follow the exfiltration in the PCAP

Approach

I built a single timeline across all three sources, lining up file access on the endpoints with outbound connections in the capture. That's what tied the activity to a specific user and machine.

ip.dst != 10.0.0.0/8 && tcp.len > 1000

The final deliverable was an investigative report and a presentation covering the evidence, the timeline, and attribution.
