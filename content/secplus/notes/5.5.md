Audits and assessments check that controls exist, work, and follow the rules. They differ by who does them and what question they answer.

### Attestation

Formal statement by a responsible party (or an auditor) that the requirements are met. For example, a SOC 2 report is an auditor's attestation about a provider's controls.

### Internal vs external

| | Internal | External |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who** | Your own staff or internal audit team | Independent third party |
| **Purpose** | Self-assessment, readiness, ongoing improvement, audit committee reporting | Independent assurance for regulators, customers, partners; required certifications |
| **Bias** | Closer knowledge, less independence | Independent, more credible, costs more |

- **Internal audits** include **compliance** checks, **audit committee** reporting, and **self-assessments**.
- **External audits** include **regulatory** examinations (government or industry regulators), **examinations** by independent auditors, and **independent third-party audits** (ISO, SOC 2, PCI QSA assessments).
- **Assessment** is a broader evaluation of security posture; **audit** compares to a defined standard and gives a conclusion.

### Penetration testing

An authorized simulated attack that tries to exploit weaknesses (see 4.3).

**Environment (what the tester knows):**

- **Known environment (white box)**: full knowledge and access.
- **Partially known (gray box)**: some information.
- **Unknown environment (black box)**: no insider information.

**Test type / what's tested:** **physical**, **offensive**, **defensive**, and **integrated** testing.

**Reconnaissance:** **passive** (public sources) and **active** (touches the target).

**Teams:** **red team** (attackers), **blue team** (defenders), **purple team** (red and blue work together to improve defenses), and **white team** (referees/coordinators).

**Rules of engagement**, **scope**, **authorization** and **reporting** are essential. Findings are ranked and tracked to remediation.

### Exam traps

- **Black box** = tester knows nothing; **white box** = knows everything.
- **Passive recon** = no direct contact with target systems.
- **Red** attacks, **blue** defends, **purple** combines both, **white** oversees.
- An **audit** compares against a standard; a **penetration test** attempts to break in.
- External audits provide independence; internal audits provide readiness.
