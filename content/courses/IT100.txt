## 0
Q: What is the 8-bit value 1011 0110 written in hexadecimal?
* 0xB6
- 0xA6
- 0xB9
- 0x6B
? Each nibble converts on its own: 1011 is B and 0110 is 6.

Q: What is decimal 200 in binary?
* 11001000
- 11000100
- 10011000
- 11010000
? 200 = 128 + 64 + 8, so bits 7, 6 and 3 are set.

Q: How many distinct values can one byte hold?
* 256
- 255
- 128
- 512
? 8 bits give 2^8 = 256 patterns (0 to 255 unsigned). 255 is the largest value, not the count.

Q: In 8-bit two's complement, what does 1111 1111 represent?
* -1
- 255
- -127
- 127
? In two's complement the top bit is the sign and all ones is -1. It is 255 only when read as unsigned.

## 1
Q: Which component holds the programs and data currently running and loses them when power is cut?
* RAM
- SSD
- UEFI firmware
- The GPU's BIOS chip
? RAM is volatile. SSDs and firmware chips keep their contents without power.

Q: What runs first when a PC powers on, initializing hardware before the OS loads?
* UEFI (or legacy BIOS) firmware
- The kernel
- The login screen
- The hypervisor
? Firmware does the power-on self test and hands off to a bootloader, which loads the kernel.

Q: Which part of the operating system manages processes, memory and access to devices?
* The kernel
- The shell
- The bootloader
- The file manager
? The shell and file manager are user programs. The kernel is the privileged core.

Q: A program wants to read a file from disk. How does it normally get the data?
* It makes a system call and the kernel does the read
- It talks to the disk controller directly
- It asks the UEFI firmware
- It edits the file table itself
? User programs are not allowed to touch hardware directly. System calls are the controlled doorway into the kernel.

## 2
Q: A 5 V supply is across a 100 ohm resistor. By Ohm's law, how much current flows?
* 0.05 A (50 mA)
- 20 A
- 0.5 A
- 500 mA
? I = V / R = 5 / 100 = 0.05 A.

Q: A device draws 2 A from a 12 V supply. How much power does it use?
* 24 W
- 6 W
- 10 W
- 14 W
? P = V x I = 12 x 2.

Q: Two resistors of 100 ohm and 220 ohm are wired in series. What is the total resistance?
* 320 ohm
- 69 ohm
- 120 ohm
- 22 000 ohm
? Series resistances add. (69 ohm would be the parallel value.)

Q: Why does an LED on a 5 V pin need a series resistor?
* Without one nothing limits the current and the LED burns out
- LEDs only work on alternating current
- The resistor raises the voltage to the LED
- The resistor makes the LED brighter
? An LED has very little resistance of its own once it conducts, so the resistor sets the current.

## 3
Q: Which component did the transistor replace in early computers?
* The vacuum tube
- Punch cards
- Magnetic tape
- The capacitor
? Transistors were smaller, cooler, cheaper and more reliable than tubes.

Q: What does Moore's observation say?
* The number of transistors on a chip roughly doubles about every two years
- CPU clock speed doubles every year
- Storage prices halve every month
- RAM size doubles every decade
? It was an observation about transistor counts, and it has slowed in recent years.

Q: The design where program code and data share one memory is named after whom?
* Von Neumann
- Harvard
- Babbage
- Turing machine tape
? Harvard architecture is the contrasting design with separate instruction and data memory.

Q: What does a CPU cache do?
* Keeps recently used data close to the core to avoid slow trips to RAM
- Stores the operating system permanently
- Encrypts data on its way to disk
- Doubles the clock speed under load
? Cache is small and fast. It works because programs reuse data they just touched.
