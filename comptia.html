how they fit

pathorderwhy
IT / help deskA+ then Network+ then Security+A+ is the standard entry ticket for help desk and desktop support. Network+ is what moves you from tickets to the network closet.
SOC analystSecurity+ then CySA+ (Network+ in parallel if packets feel shaky)Security+ gets the HR filter open. CySA+ is the analyst-level cert, and its exam is the day job: logs, indicators, vulnerability triage, incident response, reporting.
Software / dataSecurity+ only, then stopCerts matter little there. Projects and the dojo matter more. One security cert shows you can write code that does not leak.

Nate's plan: Security+ first (Q1 2027). Nothing here moves ahead of it. Network+ and A+ (Core 1 and Core 2) are built from his own notes.

how they overlap

  • Network+ and Security+ share ports, protocols, VLANs, firewalls and VPNs. Learn them once.
  • A+ and Security+ share OS hardening, malware removal, backups and data destruction.
  • CySA+ assumes Security+ and adds the analyst loop: collect, correlate, decide, write it up.
