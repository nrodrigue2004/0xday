./report --from-notes

~/report

Paste rough lab notes, get an incident report you can edit. Lines that start with a time (02:14 ...) become the timeline, lines that start with host:, ioc:, attack: fill those sections, and everything else becomes the summary. Nothing leaves your browser. Do not paste real credentials or private addresses.
