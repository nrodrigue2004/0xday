~/history

The history of computing from Ada Lovelace to quantum computers, in chapters. Each chapter goes simple, mid, complex. The last two connect it to security: the hacking timeline and the future.

History is told from public sources and simplified; dates are the commonly cited ones. Chapter 6 is opinion and labeled as such. The real code, bugs and malware are in ~/cases. Dates are also on the cheatsheet: man history.

  • [----]1: before electronics 1804 to 1945: Jacquard, Lovelace, Boole, Hollerith, Turing, Colossus, ENIACshell
  • [----]2: transistors to the PC 1947 to 1984: the transistor, Unix, C, microprocessors, the PCshell
  • [----]3: networks to the web 1969 to 1995: ARPANET, TCP/IP, email, the web, Linuxshell
  • [----]4: web, cloud, mobile 1995 to 2015: dot-com, search, smartphones, cloud, open sourceshell
  • [----]5: the AI era 2012 to now: deep learning, transformers, LLMs and agentsshell
  • [----]6: the future AI, quantum, post-quantum crypto, memory safety, and what to watch. Lab: harvest now, decrypt latershell
  • [----]7: hacking timeline 1971 to 2024: the worms, breaches and bugs that shaped security, with links to the case studiesshell

progress: [####] = all levels read (plus lab solved, if any). Saved in your browser. See also: ~/cases.
