~/detect

Turn attacker behavior into rules that fire, then hunt for what the rules miss: ATT&CK mapping, Sigma, YARA and hypothesis-driven hunts. Part of course BLU310. Each chapter goes simple, mid, complex, then a hands-on lab.

Rules and logs in the labs are fictional. Real rule repos are linked from the chapters. Commands and terms are also on the cheatsheets: man sigma, man yara.

  • [----]ATT&CK and detection basics Tactics, techniques, data sources, and how to say what a rule actually covers. Lab: map alerts to techniquesshell
  • [----]Sigma rules Write vendor-neutral log detections, tune out noise. Lab: find the rule that misses the attackshell
  • [----]YARA rules Match files and memory by strings, hex and conditions. Lab: write the condition that fitsshell
  • [----]threat hunting Hypothesis, data, pivot, document. Lab: hunt beaconing in proxy logsshell
