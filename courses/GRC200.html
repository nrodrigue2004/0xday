GRC200 Governance, risk and compliance

Frameworks (NIST CSF, 800-53, ISO 27001), risk math and the register, policy writing, audits, privacy law and professional ethics. Extends Security+ domain 5, with a risk worksheet that saves in your browser.

needs: SEC100 · 6 modules · ~7.5 h of study · not started

    ~/grc: frameworks NIST CSF

  2. 2. Risk management ~90 min

    ~/grc: risk management ~/grc: risk worksheet ~/secplus: 5.2 risk management

  3. 3. Policy writing ~60 min

    ~/grc: policy writing

  4. 4. Audits and compliance ~75 min

    ~/grc: audits and compliance ~/secplus: 5.5 audits

  5. 5. Privacy law ~75 min

# exam

20 questions drawn from the whole course. Score 80% or better to pass. If you take it before doing any modules, a pass counts as tested out, which is how placement works. Retakes are fine and the best score is kept.
