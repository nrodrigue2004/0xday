PEN300 Penetration testing

How a professional test runs: scope and rules of engagement, enumeration, Linux and Windows privilege escalation, Active Directory attack paths and detections, pivoting and reporting. Fictional networks only.

needs: SEC100, NET200, SYS300 · 7 modules · ~8.8 h of study · not started

# exam

20 questions drawn from the whole course. Score 80% or better to pass. If you take it before doing any modules, a pass counts as tested out, which is how placement works. Retakes are fine and the best score is kept.
