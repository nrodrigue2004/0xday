IT100 How computers work

Number systems, hardware, what an OS does, and just enough basic electronics to read a circuit.

needs: none · 4 modules · ~3.6 h of study · not started

# modules

  1. 1. Number systems ~45 min

    ~/basics: binary, hex and number systems

  2. 2. Hardware and operating systems ~90 min

    ~/learn: hardware diagnostics and repair ~/basics: Linux and Windows basics

  3. 3. Basic EE ~60 min

    Khan Academy: electrical engineering All About Circuits free textbook

    Voltage is push, current is flow, resistance is opposition. Ohm's law: V = I x R. Power: P = V x I. Series resistors add; parallel resistors share current. A resistor in series with an LED sets its current. Practice on a breadboard or a free simulator.

  4. 4. How we got here ~20 min

    ~/history: transistors and PCs

# exam

16 questions drawn from the whole course. Score 80% or better to pass. If you take it before doing any modules, a pass counts as tested out, which is how placement works. Retakes are fine and the best score is kept.
