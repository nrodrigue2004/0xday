CRY300 Cryptography and mobile security

How the primitives work and fail: cipher modes, RSA and ECC, TLS and PKI, classic attacks, and how mobile apps store secrets and talk to servers.

needs: SEC100 · 6 modules · ~9 h of study · not started

# modules

  1. 1. Symmetric encryption and modes ~75 min

    ~/crypto: symmetric encryption and modes ~/basics: CIA and crypto ~/learn: applied cryptography

  2. 2. RSA, ECC and signatures ~75 min

    ~/crypto: RSA, ECC and signatures

  3. 3. TLS and PKI internals ~90 min

    ~/crypto: TLS and PKI Cloudflare Learning: what is TLS

  4. 4. Attacking crypto ~120 min

    ~/crypto: attacking crypto Cryptopals challenges (free, do sets 1 and 2) Stanford Cryptography I (free course)

    Cryptopals is coding practice and goes beyond the quiz. Sets 1 and 2 cover the ECB, CBC and nonce attacks from this module.

  5. 5. Android security ~90 min

    ~/mobile: Android security OWASP MASVS

  6. 6. iOS and mobile crypto ~90 min

    ~/mobile: iOS and mobile crypto ~/learn: React Native and Expo apps ~/learn: Flutter and Firebase apps

# exam

20 questions drawn from the whole course. Score 80% or better to pass. If you take it before doing any modules, a pass counts as tested out, which is how placement works. Retakes are fine and the best score is kept.
