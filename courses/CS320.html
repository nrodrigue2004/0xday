CS320 Distributed systems

Why many machines are harder than one: replication, CAP, consensus with Raft, queues and a simulated 3-node cluster you can break.

needs: NET200 · 5 modules · ~8 h of study · not started

# modules

  1. 1. Why distributed is hard ~90 min

    ~/dist: fundamentals

  2. 2. Replication and consistency ~120 min

    ~/dist: replication (lab: the lost write)

  3. 3. Consensus and Raft ~120 min

    ~/dist: consensus (lab: the uncommitted write) Raft visualization (external)

  4. 4. Queues and streams ~90 min

    ~/dist: queues and streams (lab: the double charge)

  5. 5. Break a cluster ~60 min

    ~/dist: cluster simulator ~/learn: operating systems (processes, locks, concurrency)

    Run the five experiments on the cluster page: crash the leader, lose the majority, isolate the leader and heal, a stale read in AP mode, and last-write-wins. For each, write down what you expected before you press the button.

# exam

20 questions drawn from the whole course. Score 80% or better to pass. If you take it before doing any modules, a pass counts as tested out, which is how placement works. Retakes are fine and the best score is kept.
