CS301 Theory of computation (optional)

Light and optional: automata and regular expressions, context-free grammars, Turing machines, the halting problem and what NP means. No discrete math prerequisite.

needs: none · 3 modules · ~3 h of study · not started

# modules

  1. 1. Finite automata and regex ~75 min

    ~/langs: automata and regex regex101

  2. 2. Grammars and the stack ~45 min

    ~/langs: grammars, Turing machines and limits ~/langs: grammars and the parser

  3. 3. Computability and complexity ~60 min

    ~/langs: limits of computation Sipser's textbook

# exam

12 questions drawn from the whole course. Score 80% or better to pass. If you take it before doing any modules, a pass counts as tested out, which is how placement works. Retakes are fine and the best score is kept.
