~/grc

The paperwork half of security that decides budgets and audits: frameworks, risk math, policies, compliance, privacy law and professional ethics. Pair it with Security+ domain 5. There is a working risk worksheet that saves in your browser.

Course: GRC200 has the module checklist, quizzes and an exam. Every target here is fictional. Primary sources are free: NIST CSF and the SP 800-53 catalog. We summarize in our own words and link to the real documents.

  • [----]frameworks: NIST CSF, 800-53, ISO 27001 What each framework is for, how they map to each other and how to pick one. Lab: map a control to the right functionshell
  • [----]risk management Assets, threats, likelihood and impact, SLE/ALE, treatment options and the risk register. Lab: compute ALEshell
  • [----]policy writing Policy, standard, procedure and guideline, and how to write one people follow. Lab: fix the unenforceable policyshell
  • [----]audits and compliance SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA, evidence collection and how an audit really runs. Lab: pass or fail an audit sampleshell
  • [----]privacy law GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, FERPA, data classification and breach notification clocks. Lab: which notification deadline appliesshell
  • [----]ethics and responsible disclosure Professional codes, the CFAA, authorization, disclosure timelines and safe harbor. Lab: judge the researcher's choicesshell
  • [tool]risk worksheet Score risks, compute ALE and keep a register that saves in this browsertool

progress: [####] = all levels read (plus lab solved, if any). Saved in your browser. Reference: man grc
