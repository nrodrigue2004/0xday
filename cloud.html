Cloud basics for AWS and Azure: the concepts they share, then each platform, then security. Each chapter goes simple, mid, complex, and most end in a hands-on lab.

No lab here touches a real account. The labs are simulated, and the last chapter covers free-tier practice with billing guardrails. Commands and terms are also on the cheatsheets: man cloud, man aws, man az.
