3.2 Troubleshooting Mobile Devices

study notes

Apps and Updates

  • App won't launch or is slow: restart the device (clears RAM/cache), force close (iOS: swipe up from app switcher; Android: Settings > Apps > Force stop), update, reinstall.
  • App won't update: update manually/Update all; check payment method (valid card even for free apps), storage, internet connectivity, MDM policy blocking installs, app store maintenance, restart.
  • OS update fails: storage (OS updates are large), bandwidth, network filtering (try another network), restart.

Hardware and Radios

  • Battery drain: poor signal makes the radio keep searching (use airplane mode), aging battery (replace), disable unused Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/GPS, check Battery health and usage in Settings.
  • Random reboots: update OS and apps, check battery/hardware health; support reads crash logs. Often a hardware fault, replace.
  • Wi-Fi: be closer to AP or try another AP, confirm Wi-Fi is on, verify security key/settings, restart.
  • Bluetooth: confirm enabled, toggle off/on, re-pair, restart.
  • NFC: toggle, reset, check payment cards/wallet.
  • AirDrop: needs Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, proximity, and discoverability set to Contacts Only or Everyone (temporarily).
  • Autorotate: G-sensor/accelerometer; check rotation lock, restart app/device, else hardware support.

flashcards

self-check

1. A free app won't download, store shows an expired card. Fix:

  • a) Update payment method
  • b) Factory reset
  • c) Replace battery
  • d) Disable NFC
answer

a) Store requires valid payment.

2. Phone dies by noon while stationary in a poor-coverage area. Best fix:

  • a) Airplane mode / disable unused radios
  • b) New case
  • c) Disable updates
  • d) Reset network
answer

a) Radio search drains battery.

3. Employee cannot install an app on a corporate phone. Check:

  • a) MDM policy
  • b) Payment
  • c) Screen rotation
  • d) AirDrop
answer

a) Policy block.

short lab: Phone won't update

List five checks for an OS update that stalls at download.

walkthrough

Storage space, battery/power, Wi-Fi quality and bandwidth, network filtering (try another network), then restart and retry.
