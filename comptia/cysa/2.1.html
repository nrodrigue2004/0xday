CS0-004domain 2.0 · Vulnerability Management2.1

2.1 Given a scenario, implement the appropriate vulnerability scanning method

study notes

Scanning finds known weaknesses. It is not a pentest, and it lies in both directions, so you validate.

Before you scan

  • Asset inventory: you cannot scan what you do not know exists. Include shadow IT, cloud, containers and IoT.
  • Planning: scheduling (off-peak, change windows), operations (notify owners, avoid critical processes), performance (bandwidth, scan throttling, fragile devices like OT or old printers), sensitivity levels (what data may a credentialed scan touch), segmentation (scanner placement so firewalls do not hide hosts), regulatory (PCI DSS requires quarterly external scans by an ASV).

Scan types

  • Internal vs external: internal sees what an insider sees, external what the internet sees.
  • Agent vs agentless: agents on hosts report continuously, work for roaming laptops and need install and updates. Agentless scans over the network.
  • Credentialed vs non-credentialed: credentialed logs in and sees missing patches, configs and local software, with far fewer false positives. Non-credentialed is the outsider view.
  • Passive vs active: passive listens to traffic (safe for OT, slow, misses quiet hosts). Active sends probes (thorough, can disrupt).
  • Discovery: mapping scans find hosts and topology; device fingerprinting guesses OS and services.
  • Baseline scanning: compare configuration to a standard such as CIS benchmarks, PCI DSS, or the ISO 27000 series.

Exam habit: pick "credentialed" for accuracy, "passive" for fragile systems, "agent" for remote laptops, "external" for the attack surface view.

flashcards

self-check

1. Scan results are full of false positives for missing patches. Which change improves accuracy most?

  • a) Non-credentialed scan
  • b) Credentialed scan
  • c) External scan
  • d) Fewer plugins
answer

b) Authenticated scanning checks the actual patch level.

2. A plant's PLC network cannot tolerate probes. Best method?

  • a) Aggressive active scan
  • b) Passive monitoring
  • c) Credentialed full scan
  • d) No scanning ever
answer

b) Passive observation avoids disrupting devices.

short lab: Plan a scan

You must scan a 200-host office network plus 15 remote laptops, including a legacy label printer that crashes under heavy probing. Pick scan types and settings.

walkthrough

Credentialed internal active scan for servers and desktops during a window, with throttled rates. Agent-based for the laptops. Exclude or lightly/passively check the printer. External scan of the public IPs separately. Notify owners, document the exceptions, and compare the config to CIS baselines.
