SY0-701domain 5.0 · Security Program Management & Oversight5.3

5.3 Explain the processes associated with third-party risk assessment and management

key ideas

  • Vendor assessment: penetration testing, right-to-audit clause, evidence of internal audits, independent assessments, supply chain analysis.
  • Vendor selection: due diligence, conflict of interest. Monitoring and questionnaires, rules of engagement.
  • Agreements: SLA, MOA, MOU, MSA, WO/SOW, NDA, BPA.

study notes

You inherit the risks of everyone you do business with. This objective covers how to assess vendors and the agreements that bind them.

Third-party risk assessment

  • Vendor assessment: judge suppliers before and during the relationship.
    • Penetration testing (with permission) and right-to-audit clauses allow verification.
    • Evidence of internal audits and independent assessments (SOC 2 reports, ISO 27001 certification).
    • Supply chain analysis: who are *their* suppliers? (fourth parties)
    • Vendor questionnaires about security practices, incident history and controls.
  • Vendor selection: due diligence (investigate before signing), conflict of interest check.
  • Vendor monitoring: continuous review of performance, security posture and incidents. Reassess periodically.
  • Vendor viability, financial stability, and geographic location (laws and data sovereignty).
  • Questionnaires, rules of engagement for tests, and right to audit are standard.
  • Offboarding / termination: return or destroy data, revoke access, confirm deletion.

Agreement types

AgreementPurposeKey features
SLA (service level agreement)Defines the level of service a provider will deliverMeasurable targets such as 99.9% uptime, response times, penalties
MOA (memorandum of agreement)Formal agreement on how parties will work togetherStronger than an MOU; sets responsibilities
MOU (memorandum of understanding)Statement of shared intentGenerally non-binding; informal
MSA (master service agreement)Umbrella contract with standing termsLater work orders reference it, speeding up new projects
WO / SOW (work order / statement of work)Defines a specific projectScope, deliverables, deadlines, acceptance criteria, cost
NDA (non-disclosure agreement)Keeps information confidentialLegal consequences for sharing; mutual or one-way
BPA (business partners agreement)Sets how partners operate togetherProfit/loss sharing, responsibilities, exit

Contracts often add security requirements, breach notification timelines, audit rights, data handling and termination clauses.

Exam traps

  • SLA is about *service levels*; SOW is about *project scope*; MSA is the *master* terms.
  • MOU is non-binding; MOA is more formal and binding.
  • NDA protects confidentiality.
  • Third-party risk includes fourth parties (your vendor's vendors).
  • You can outsource work, not accountability.
