IT

Support, administration and infrastructure. The entry point for most tech careers.

  • 22qIT Help Desk / Support Technician The front line of IT.
  • 22qSystems Administrator You run the servers, accounts, backups and updates that everyone else assumes will just work.

ls ~/careers/cyber

Cyber

Defense, investigation and offense. Most start with an IT or SOC foundation.

  • 23qSOC Analyst (Tier 1 / Tier 2) You watch the alerts, decide which are real, and stop incidents early.
  • 20qIncident Responder / DFIR Analyst When something real happens, you contain it, work out what the attacker did, and help the business recover.
  • 22qPenetration Tester / Red Team You are paid, with permission, to break into systems and report how.

ls ~/careers/dev

Software / Data

Building software, testing it and working with data.

  • 25qSoftware Developer You design, write, test and maintain software.
  • 21qQA / Software Tester You find the problems before users do, and increasingly you write the automation that does it.
  • 22qData / SQL Analyst You turn messy data into answers people can act on.
