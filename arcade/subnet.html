You have 60 seconds. Answer subnetting questions as fast as you can: masks, prefixes, usable hosts, network and broadcast addresses, and whether two hosts share a subnet. Every 4 right answers moves you up a level (harder prefixes). A wrong answer costs 3 seconds and shows the working so you can learn the trick.
